نفوذNFOUZ Concept Sheet v2

This is the semi-realistic heroic direction the owner approved after rejecting v1. The cast are adults, about 8 heads tall, in grounded tactical gear, with worn military-grade weapons and cinematic night lighting. Gulf near-future identity shows up as gear details: shemagh weave, the bisht cut with brass trim, and mashrabiya lattice as grilles and engraving. Every number on this page is copied from the code.

Read this first. These SVG drawings set a direction: proportions, silhouettes, materials, palette and lighting. They are not production art and cannot be dropped into Unity. A premium battle-game look needs sculpted and textured 3D models (15–25k tris per hero, 2048² PBR textures) and properly lit renders. Specs and sourcing are in art_bible_v2.md.

How combat looks شكل المعركة

The top-down camera frames about 10 m across (≈39 px per metre on a 390 pt screen). The arena radius is 15 m (ArenaRadiusMeters). The match lasts 90 s plus 30 s of overtime. The HUD is restyled to the serious palette: dark glass, thin strokes, no candy fills.
