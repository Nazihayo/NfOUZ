Classes الفئات

Each class has one shape: triangle, tall rectangle, square. Each must read from the battle camera's top-down view before colour helps. HP/EN come from BattleManager.GetClassBaseStats, abilities from ClassAbilityDatabase. The weapon each one holds is a visual pairing only, because code lets any class use any weapon.

How combat looks شكل المعركة

Top-down camera framing about 10 m across (≈39 px per metre on a 390 pt screen), so the Pulse Arc's 3 m reach fills a third of the screen. The arena is a 15 m radius circle (ArenaRadiusMeters). The match lasts 90 s, plus 30 s of overtime. Moment shown: a Scout with Pulse Blade winds up Pulse Arc on a Titan.

  1. 1
    Floating touch stick, lower-left 40% التحريكIt appears where the thumb lands (TouchStick) and never sits on UI. The dashed zone is a design guide only and is not drawn in-game.
  2. 2
    Four action buttons هجوم · خاصة · مراوغة · قدرةThese are Attack (largest), Special, Dodge and Class ability, the four bits in CombatActionInput. Cyan badges show the energy cost. The Special shows the 8 s cooldown sweep and the ability glows in the class accent when ready. Icons are white glyphs so one sprite serves all classes. The device only reports intent; the host resolves the outcome.
  3. 3
    HP / EN bars الصحة · الطاقة"You" sits on the right in the Arabic layout. Each bar is anchored at its avatar and drains toward the centre, so yours fills from the right. HP is coral and EN is cyan, the reserved colours. The values shown are the Scout's 80/120 and the Titan's 140/80.
  4. 4
    Match timer90 s (SessionMaxDurationSeconds), then "وقت إضافي!" for a 30 s overtime. The gold hex frame is the influence colour.
  5. 5
    Wind-up telegraphA white 35% wedge with the real geometry: 140° and 3 m for Pulse Arc. A cyan arc grows across the 0.45 s wind-up. White reads on every faction colour.
  6. 6
    Faction ring + facing chevronThe only place faction colour appears on a combatant (Falcons blue here, Titans red opposite). The chevron replaces today's Facing cube.
  7. 7
    Arena edge, 15 m radiusAn emissive cyan ring with mashrabiya pylons. Outside is dimmed, with Gulf rooftops for context. Shadow Lunge and Blink Step stop at this line.
  • The Arabic class, weapon and consumable names are proposals, because StringTable.cs has no keys for them yet. They need a native reviewer. «الصامد» was chosen for the Titan class to avoid colliding with the Titans faction «العمالقة».
  • Health and energy values come from BattleManager.GetClassBaseStats (the remote-player path). The local player uses PlayerSession.MaxHealth/MaxEnergy from the server.
  • These are concept illustrations, not production assets. The specs, budgets and the pink-capsule fix are in art_bible.md.
